# Credits

- **Author:** Malikor
- **Design and code:** Malikor, with Claude (Anthropic) as coding assistant.
- **Reference for which stats to collect and how to present them:** the Blackthornes addons for WoW: Forever (Farmer, Tradeskills, Equipment, Butler, Pets) by Blackthornes. Only their public descriptions were consulted; no code, art or visual style from them is used.
- **Dashboard layout inspiration:** a generic analytics-dashboard concept image supplied by Malikor (layout ideas only; the Odometer theme, icons and charts are original).
- **Feature ideas (public descriptions only, no code):** AutoBiographer, Played Plus, TrackMe, Ultra Statistics, Hardcore Stat Companion, SimpleAltTracker, Exlist, Accountwide Stats, Warbandeer: Characters, VanillaDashboard, TrackStatsPlus, JemiStats, Siouxx Classic Stats, Classic Score, PvP Stats Tracker, Extended Character Stats, PlayedTracker, TimePlayed+, Altoholic, CharacterStatsClassic, Tally, PlayerTracker, Haul, Kill Census, Details!.
- **Theme matching:** ElvUI and Tukui (Tukui team) colour and font conventions; EllesmereUI's public skinning API (SKINNING_API.md, EllesmereGaming/EllesmereUI). Only their public interfaces are read at runtime; no files are bundled.
- **Dungeon and raid data:** Warcraft Tavern's World of Warcraft: Forever dungeon and raid guides (level ranges, locations, boss lists). Forever dungeon boss names and encounter IDs checked against DBM-Dungeons' `DBM-Party-Forever` encounter scaffolds (DeadlyBossMods); facts only, no code.
- **Forever client API:** the captured API surface and live-client findings in Thunderz96/forever-addon-kit (MIT), used by `scripts/api_scan.py` during development; nothing from it is bundled.
- **Forever addon restrictions:** findings shared by the authors of Field Guide (cjber/field-guide-forever issue #91) and the WoW Forever addon restriction notes on wowforevertierlist.com, which pointed to the combat log being secure-only.
- **API references:** [warcraft.wiki.gg](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/), [Gethe/wow-ui-source](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source).
- **Artwork:** icon, logo, sidebar glyphs and dot texture drawn for this addon. The Lifetime Stats and Stats Explorer frame textures (parchment, wood, frame rails, gold corners, title plaque, compass emblem, crests, menu highlight, divider) are generated by `scripts/make_framed_textures.py`; their layout follows mock-ups supplied by the user, and no artwork from the mock-ups is used. The overview's milestone and highlight icons are the game's own icons. The Stone & Gold and Adventure Journal textures (stone, leather, rails, corner plates, portrait ring, highlight bar) are generated by `scripts/make_styled_textures.py`. Those skins are styled after the Altoholic and Dungeon Journal addons; no code or artwork from either is used. The Adventurer's Notebook textures (paper, wood, spiral ring, tab, tag, fold, seal) are generated by `scripts/make_notebook_textures.py` and its 19 ink sketches by `scripts/make_notebook_sketches.py`; the layout idea of a tabbed adventurer's notebook was suggested by the user from a printed D&D planner, but no artwork from it is used. Logo/icon text uses Inter (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and DejaVu Sans Mono (Bitstream Vera license). The in-game window uses the game's own fonts and the standard minimap border texture.
